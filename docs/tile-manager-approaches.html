✓ Pros

  • Clean separation of concerns — tile management vs. pack curation are distinct modes
  • Pack View grid is spatial and satisfying — you can see the whole pack at a glance
  • Rarity breakdown bar gives instant pack health feedback
  • All Tiles tab maps directly to the "master tile pool" mental model
  • Tabs match how you think: "I want to manage a tile" vs. "I want to review a pack"

✗ Cons

  • Adding a tile to a pack requires going to All Tiles first, then toggling membership — can't do it from Pack View directly
  • Two navigation paths to the same tile (from either tab)
