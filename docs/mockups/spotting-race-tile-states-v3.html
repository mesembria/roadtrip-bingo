05 At a Glance — Both Perspectives

Your View

  • Unclaimed → cream image zone, dark band, fuse live
  • You tap → green image zone, undo chip in band, countdown ring, fuse paused
  • You finalize → Spotted +N pts · You stamp drops at −12°
  • Opp. pending → greyscale image + 🔒 + opponent name in band, not tappable
  • Opp. claimed → Taken +N pts · Sarah stamp at +9°, tile dims
  • Conflict lost → ✕ flash in image zone, then tile locks to opponent
  • Expiring → red border pulse, fuse almost empty
  • New tile → slides up, full fuse bar

Opponent's View (of your actions)

  • You tap → tile immediately greys + 🔒 + your name in band
  • You undo → lock lifts, fuse resumes, fully claimable again
  • You finalize → Taken +N pts · Philippe amber stamp
  • Expiry events → host-driven, same on both devices
  • New tile → same slide-in animation, identical timing
  • Conflict win (you lost) → no special state; they just see your lock prevail
