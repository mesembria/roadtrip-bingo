At a Glance — What Each Player Sees Per State

Your View

  • Unclaimed → normal tile, expiry bar live
  • You tap → green tint, undo chip, 2s ring
  • Finalized → +N pts ✓ green flash, tile held ~0.4s
  • Opp. pending → grey + 🔒 + their name, not tappable
  • Opp. finalized → Sarah +4 amber flash, then gone
  • Conflict lost → ✕ red flash, tile still locked by opp.
  • Expiring → red border pulse when <3s left
  • New tile → slides up from bottom of slot

Opponent's View (of your actions)

  • You tap → grey + 🔒 + your name immediately
  • You undo → lock lifts, tile back to normal
  • You finalize → +N pts amber flash, tile fades out
  • Expiry events → same as your view (host-driven)
  • New tile → same slide-in animation, same timing
  • No claim denied state for opponent — they just see the lock and know to move on
